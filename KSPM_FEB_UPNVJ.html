01 / ABOUT KSPM

A Space to Learn.
A Community to Grow.

The Capital Market Study Group of the Faculty of Economics and Business, UPN “Veteran” Jakarta, is a student community dedicated to learning about capital markets, strengthening analytical skills, and gaining experience through education, research, competitions, and collaboration.

We believe financial knowledge goes beyond theory. It is about having the confidence to analyze, make informed decisions, and keep growing.

ConnectGrow together
GrowCreate impact
OUR PURPOSE

Learn. Connect. Grow.

Empowering students to understand the market, think critically, and turn knowledge into meaningful opportunities.
