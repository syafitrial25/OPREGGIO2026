GET TO KNOW US

More Than Just an Organization.

Kelompok Studi Pasar Modal (KSPM) FEB UPN Veteran Jakarta is a student organization dedicated to developing knowledge, skills, and experience in the capital market and investment industry.

We provide opportunities for students to learn, explore, collaborate, and develop their potential through various educational programs and organizational experiences.

Our Vision & Mission

01

Our Vision

Terwujudnya Kelompok Studi Pasar Modal Fakultas Ekonomi dan Bisnis Universitas Pembangunan Nasional "Veteran" Jakarta yang intelektual dan terampil dalam bidang pasar modal.

02

Our Mission

  • Menjadi pusat edukasi dan informasi pasar modal.
  • Menerapkan praktik-praktik ilmu dalam pasar modal.
  • Meningkatkan kompetensi mahasiswa dalam bidang pasar modal.
